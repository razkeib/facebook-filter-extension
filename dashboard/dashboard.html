Advanced Search Guide

Boolean Operators

  • AND / &&: crypto AND btc
  • Implicit AND: crypto btc
  • OR / ||: crypto OR stock
  • NOT / !: NOT scam
  • Grouping: (crypto OR btc) AND NOT scam

Exact Strings

  • Wrap in quotes: "free shipping"

Field Targeting

  • author:"Name"
  • group:"Name"
  • text:keyword

Regular Expressions (regex)

  • Wrap in slashes: /b[aeiou]y/i
  • Combined: author:/smith/i AND /hiring/
